Sniglet for display, Zen Maru Gothic for text. Timers in Zen Maru Gothic Black with fixed-width cells, sitting inside a pressed dial ring that shows progress.
Backdrop
No backdrop: the page is the surface. A couple of petals drift slowly across it. Calm is the point.
Motion character
Gentle and breathing: shadows swell and recede, things rise softly, Won sinks in like a thumb into foam.
Ornament / mascot
Puff, an embossed cloud who peeks over the summary. Tiny dot-eyed faces on status discs (sleepy, focused, content). Petals instead of confetti.

Tick
Changed digits rise 8px out of a soft blur and settle (450ms ease-out). The running face sends out one slow peach halo per second, like a heartbeat.
Won
The row's shadow flattens and inverts, sinking it into the surface; a mint ring blooms outward and a few petals float off. Then it softly reappears, pressed-in, at the bottom.
Reduced motion · Sound
Static: pressed-in row, happy face, check icon, "Won" word. Sound (muted): tick = barely-there felt tap, Won = soft marimba note, Complete = gentle music-box phrase.
Dialect lean
Workspace
Its natural home: a calm, low-glare desk for long runs. Faces and Puff stay small; contrast comes from ink text, not shadows.
Broadcast
Needs help over games: opaque panel, white + ink keyline, bolder digits. The quietest Overlay of the seven, by design.
Showcase
Spectacle through softness: huge embossed shapes slowly rising out of the page as you scroll, Puff floating between sections.
