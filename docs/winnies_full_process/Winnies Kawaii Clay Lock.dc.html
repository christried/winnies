Material · fondant clay
Every surface is a matte, fully rounded pastel slab sitting on a solid lip: an unblurred shadow 0 8px 0 -2px (12px on hero cards) in a slightly darker tint of the surface. On top of it go a soft drop 0 16px 26px -12px rgba(220,80,140,.35), a top inner highlight inset 0 5px 7px rgba(255,255,255,.9) and a faint bottom inner shade. Surfaces carry a 6px speckle grain (rgba(74,43,58,.05) dots), and hero cards add one masked fingerprint whorl (concentric 1.5px rings at 3.5% opacity) in a corner. Pressing moves an element down by its lip height and collapses the lip. Wells (inputs, progress bar, total timer) are the inverse, inset 0 4px 8px rgba(220,100,150,.22). Radii are pill or ≥38px; nothing has a sharp corner.

Type · Mochiy + M PLUS Rounded
Mochiy Pop One (400) for display, Winnie titles, game names, buttons and every timer digit. M PLUS Rounded 1c (500 / 700 / 800) for body, win conditions and meta. Material Symbols Rounded for icons, filled on active states. Scale: Winnie title 54/1.08, total timer 66, section heads 22, row game 21/1.45, row timer 22, body 15–17. Timer digits sit in fixed-width slots (0.7em per digit, 0.36em per colon, colons in the muted colour) so ticking never shifts the layout. Single-line Mochiy labels need line-height ≥1.4 because its descenders hang low. Both families cover Japanese, which helps later localisation.
Backdrop · strawberry dots
Polka dots: radial-gradient(#FFCADD 18%, transparent 19%) tiled at 52px, with a second layer offset 26px 26px, over Milk #FFE3EC (dark: #2E1B29 dots on #231520). A full-page speckle sits on top: rgba(74,43,58,.05) 1px dots every 6px plus rgba(255,255,255,.18) every 9px. The app sits on a 48px-radius sheet. No gradients, no floating decorations, no extra patterns. The Overlay has no backdrop at all; it's transparent over gameplay.

Motion · jelly physics
Every move squashes on take-off (scale 1.1, .9), stretches in the air (.92, 1.1), squashes on landing, then settles. Springs use cubic-bezier(.3,1.7,.5,1). Tick (1/s): changed total digits hop 12px with a squash (480ms); changed row digits flick up 7px with a −6° tilt (380ms). Running faces and Nini beat once per tick in their fatigue style: hop, nod, wobble, slow droop (950ms), side shake, stepped vibrate. Won: the row jellies (700ms), a fountain of ♥ ★ ● ✿ in the accent colours bursts up, Nini cheers for 1.8s, and the row drops into the Won group from −40px with a squash (600ms). With reduced motion all of this stops; faces and colours still show state.
Mascot & ornament · Nini + faces
Nini is a white mochi (radius 50% 50% 44% 44% / 62% 62% 38% 38%, radial #FFFFFF → #FFEFF5 → #FAD0DF, lip #F2B3CB). She lives in the Winnie header card and her mood follows the Winnie (section 4). Every challenge has a clay face disc (54px in the Workspace, 44px in the Overlay) drawn in Cocoa with a white catch-light. The face colour shows status and how long it has been running: Sky = ready, Butter = snoozing, a running ramp from #FFA6CB through #FF92BF, #FF8BB5, #E7A9C8 and #FF8296 to #FF6372 at 20 min, 1 h, 2 h, 3 h and 4 h, and Matcha when Won. Fatigue adds one sign per stage: sweat drops #8FD3FF, eye bags, an anger mark #E0245E, steam. The only other ornaments are Lilac washi tape on Pinned rows and a Butter star sticker on Won faces. No hearts in the UI.

w Small sizes / app icon: the single Bubblegum “w” with its lip on a Surface tile. Below 24px tall, drop the extrusion to one 2px step.
Usage
• Lowercase “winnies” in Mochiy Pop One. Each letter keeps its fixed colour, darker lip and tilt (values above). Never re-order, re-colour or straighten them. • Extrusion: three solid lip steps at 5%, 10% and 15% of the cap height, plus one soft shadow. No outline and no sticker border. • The same mark works on Milk and on Night. Never place it on a pastel accent or over gameplay; in the Overlay the Winnie title replaces it. • Clear space = the height of the “n” on all sides. Minimum 24px tall (nav is 40px). • Idle motion: letters hop in a wave (−14px, 110ms stagger, every 2.6s) in the nav and on Showcase only. Static everywhere else and for reduced motion. • Showcase may scale it to ≥100px. The Workspace nav uses 40px. It never appears twice on one screen.
4 · Nini (kept)
Mood = state of the Winnie
{{ m.name }} {{ m.rule }}
Usage
• Place: the Winnie header card in the Workspace (180px), plus Showcase. Never in the Overlay, nav, rows or menus. One Nini per screen. • Mood comes from the Winnie: paused = Snooze; while running it follows the longest-running challenge’s fatigue stage; any Won = Cheer for 1.8s; all Won = Party. • Idle: 2.2s jelly breathing. While running she also beats on every tick in her stage’s style. Party = two −90px jumps and confetti, once. • Accessories show state: Bubblegum headband while running (it slips when tired), Butter crown on Complete. No other props or outfits. • Minimum 96px wide. Face features always Cocoa #4A2B3A. On Night the white mochi stays white; don’t tint her to the theme. • Later: hovering Nini opens a speech bubble tailored to the user’s status. No speech bubble in v1.
