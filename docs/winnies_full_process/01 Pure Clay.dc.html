Type
Fredoka for display, Figtree for text. Total Timer digits sit on their own fixed-width clay tiles with clay-bead colons; row timers use fixed-width digit cells so nothing jumps.
Backdrop
A warm cream tabletop with slow, blurred clay-coloured glows and a few floating pebbles. No texture, no noise: the objects carry all the detail.
Motion character
Springy squash-and-stretch: everything is a little heavy, overshoots once and settles with a wobble.
Ornament / mascot
Clay beads, pebbles, rings and pills. No mascot: the squishy shapes are the characters, and Won confetti is made of the same beads.
