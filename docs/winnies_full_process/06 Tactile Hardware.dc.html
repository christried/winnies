TICK
Changed segments refresh with a two-frame LCD flicker (140ms, stepped); the analog dial hand jumps one detent per second; the running LED blinks red.
WON
The toggle snaps over, the row jolts like a latch engaging, paper-strip confetti spits out, LED goes green, and the slot re-seats at the bottom like a cartridge.
REDUCED MOTION · SOUND
Static: LED colour + WON word + toggle position. Sound (muted): tick = quartz click, Won = ka-chunk latch + beep, Complete = receipt printer whirr and a triple beep.
